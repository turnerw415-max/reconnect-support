Privacy Policy

Read our full Privacy Policy for what information we collect, how it is stored, and your rights. It is also available in-app under Profile → Privacy Policy.

In short: your case notes, case toolkit entries, and document-vault files are private to your account and protected by row-level security in our database. Community posts, comments, and profile information you choose to share are visible to signed-in Re-connect members as part of the Community service. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. Contact us at the email above for any privacy questions or data requests.

Re-connect is an independent app and is not affiliated with HM Courts & Tribunals Service, the Child Maintenance Service, or any government body.
