Privacy Policy

Reconnect's full privacy policy is available in-app under Profile → Privacy Policy, and covers what information we collect, how it's stored, and your rights.

In short: your account email, profile details, and anything you choose to enter (case notes, documents, community posts) are private to your account and protected by row-level security in our database. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. Contact us at the email above for any privacy questions or data requests.

Reconnect is an independent app and is not affiliated with HM Courts & Tribunals Service, the Child Maintenance Service, or any government body.
