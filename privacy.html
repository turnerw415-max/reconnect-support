Re-connect Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Re-connect is a support application designed to help people navigating parental alienation, family separation and the family court process. We take the privacy and security of our users seriously.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Re-connect, we may collect information such as:

  • Your email address and account information.
  • Profile information you choose to provide.
  • Case notes and information you choose to save within the app.
  • Documents or files you choose to upload.
  • Community posts or other content you choose to share.
  • Technical information necessary to operate, secure and improve the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and operate Re-connect.
  • Authenticate and manage user accounts.
  • Store information and content requested by users.
  • Provide app features and community functionality.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the service.
  • Respond to support, privacy and account requests.
  • Improve the app and resolve technical problems.

3. Sensitive Information

Users may choose to enter information relating to family circumstances, court proceedings or other personal matters. Users should only provide information they are comfortable storing within the service.

4. Data Storage and Security

Re-connect uses appropriate technical and organisational measures intended to protect user information. Access to private user data is restricted through account authentication and database security controls, including row-level security where applicable.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, but reasonable measures are used to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

5. Sharing of Information

Re-connect does not sell users' personal information or use personal information for third-party advertising.

Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the app, such as hosting, database, authentication and infrastructure providers. Information may also be disclosed where required by law.

6. Community Content

Information that you deliberately publish through community features may be visible to other users. You should avoid posting confidential court documents, addresses, children's private information or other sensitive personal information publicly.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain an account, meet legal obligations or resolve security and operational issues.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of certain processing.

To make a privacy or data request, contact: reconnectsupportapp@gmail.com

9. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Re-connect account and associated personal information by contacting reconnectsupportapp@gmail.com. We will process legitimate deletion requests subject to any information we are legally required or permitted to retain.

10. Children's Privacy

Re-connect is not intended for use by children. Users should take particular care not to publish children's personal or identifying information through community features.

11. Legal and Professional Disclaimer

Re-connect provides information and support resources. It is not a law firm and does not provide legal representation. Information within the app should not be treated as a substitute for advice from a qualified legal professional where professional advice is required.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Re-connect develops. The date at the top of this page will show when the policy was last updated.

13. Contact Us

For privacy questions, account requests or other enquiries, contact:

reconnectsupportapp@gmail.com
